*Wolbachia* can induce parthenogenesis in its host, which can facilitate its own transmission. So far, this induction has only been proven in haplodiploid species. However, its presence in *Nephus voeltzkowi* could indicate a novel host taxon. Additionally, I am investigating the role of *Wolbachia* in the maintenance of parthenogenetic lineages of *Aptinothrips* grass thrip species where I have shown widespread horizontal transmission of the infection.
